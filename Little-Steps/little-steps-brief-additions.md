# Little Steps — Brief Review & Suggested Additions

*Every little step is a big milestone.*

---

## Part 1: Gaps in the Current Brief

- **Priorities:** Everything reads as equally important. Mark what's in version 1 (profiles, milestones, growth, basic memories) and what comes later (PDF recaps, family sharing, printed books). Otherwise the first quote or build will be huge.
- **Milestone source:** "Reliable references" is vague. Name one, such as the CDC's "Learn the Signs. Act Early." checklists or the WHO motor milestones.
- **Growth charts:** WHO standards cover 0–5 years, which matches the app's age range. Specify how a measurement is shown, e.g. a percentile line with no "normal/abnormal" label, so it doesn't drift into diagnosis.
- **Sharing:** Define who can see or edit, such as a partner as co-owner and grandparents as view-only, and how someone gets invited.
- **Free plan limits:** "Limited memory entries" needs a number (e.g. 50 photos). So does the premium storage cap.
- **Philippines:** Name the Data Privacy Act of 2012 directly, decide on a Filipino language option, and decide whether pricing is in pesos.
- **Content owner:** Decide who writes and reviews the Discover articles, e.g. a partner pediatrician.

## Part 2: What a Mom Would Add

### Daily Care (what parents open the app for most)
- **Feeding log:** breast side and duration, bottle amount, pumping, and a "last fed" timer on the home screen.
- **Sleep and diapers:** quick one-tap logging. Pediatricians ask about wet diapers in the early weeks.
- **One-handed mode:** big buttons, a dark night mode, no bright white screens during night feeds.

### Health Records
- **Vaccines:** a tracker that follows the Philippine schedule (DOH/PPS), plus a place to photograph the baby book card.
- **Sick days:** temperature, medicines given and at what time (so caregivers don't double-dose), and symptoms.
- **Doctor visits:** a "questions for my pedia" list to add to all week, then notes from the check-up.
- **Allergies and teething:** a log for new foods with reactions, and a tooth chart.

### Emotional Side
- **Gentle wording:** instead of "Not yet," use something like "Still working on it." The app never shows red warnings or compares a baby to others.
- **Mom's own wellbeing:** a postpartum check-in and links to support, not just baby content.
- **Letters to my child:** sealed messages to open on their 18th birthday.
- **Voice notes:** record first babbles and laughs, not just photos and video.

### Family Life
- **Shared caregiving:** partner, lola, or yaya can log feeds and naps on their own phones, with each entry showing who logged it.
- **Pregnancy mode:** bump photos, ultrasounds, and the birth story, carried over once the baby arrives.
- **Sibling view:** compare "firsts" dates side by side across children (private, just for fun).

### Local and Practical
- **Filipino touches:** Tagalog option, binyag (baptism) and first-birthday templates, prices in pesos, GCash payment.
- **Offline-first:** spotty signal shouldn't stop logging.
- **Backup peace of mind:** a clear "your photos are safe" status.
- **Physical keepsake:** order a printed photo book from inside the app.

## Part 3: Suggested Version 1 Scope

**Launch with:**
- Baby profiles (with corrected age)
- Milestone tracker with gentle wording
- Growth tracker (WHO charts)
- Basic memories and Firsts Collection
- Feeding / sleep / diaper logging
- Vaccine tracker (Philippine schedule)
- Shared caregiving

**Save for later:**
- PDF/monthly recaps and printed photo books
- Pregnancy mode
- Letters to my child, voice notes
- Sibling comparison
- Postpartum check-in content
